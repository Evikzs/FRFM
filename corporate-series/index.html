The event

Table soccer, company against company

The Romanian Table Soccer Federation organises a table soccer match series for people who work in companies. Each company sends one or more teams. The teams play a series of matches against teams from other companies.

It is a simple way to get colleagues out of the office, build a team and meet people from other companies. You do not need experience to take part.

Who can join

Companies

Send one or more teams to represent your company in the series.

Teams and departments

Use the series as a teambuilding activity for your team, without the rest of the company.

Groups of colleagues

A good reason to start going out together and to play on a regular basis.

Questions

Before you sign up

Do our players need table soccer experience?

No. The series is open to all levels. We explain the rules before the first match.

How many people can a company send?

Give us an estimate in the form. A company can send one team or more. We confirm the final number with you.

Can one team from a company join on its own?

Yes. A department, a project team or a group of colleagues can register without the rest of the company. Use it as a teambuilding activity, or as a reason to go out together.

When and where is the event?

The date and the location are not set yet. We will announce them on this page and on our Facebook page. Registered contact persons hear first.

Is this a one-time event?

For now, yes. If interest grows, we will organise the series more often. Tick “Keep me informed about future editions” in the form to hear about them.

Does the form commit my company to anything?

No. The form only tells us that you are interested. We contact you, and you decide after you have all the details.

What do you do with my details?

We use them only to contact you about the series. We do not share them with other parties. You can ask us to delete them at any time.
